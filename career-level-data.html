Key takeaway: Senior roles dominate with 67,400 openings. The mid-to-senior jump ($95K→$135K, 42%) is the best salary move. Manager ($148K) comes before Lead ($155K). For department-level salary ranges, see the salary data with standard deviations.

Methodology

Career level data is derived from 285,000+ open job listings across 10,000+ company career pages. Seniority levels (C-Suite through Entry Level) are classified from job titles using pattern matching on keywords like "VP," "Director," "Senior," "Lead," and "Manager." Salary medians represent the midpoint of posted salary ranges for jobs with disclosed compensation (21,000+ listings). Work arrangement classification (Remote, Hybrid, On-site) is extracted from job descriptions and location fields. Department classification uses title and description keyword mapping across 15 functional categories. All metrics refresh daily.
