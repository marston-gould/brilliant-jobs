Frequently Asked Questions

Which seniority level has the most openings?↓

Senior at 67,400, Mid-Level 56,200, Entry 42,100. C-Suite has just 2,800 (<1%).

What is the biggest salary jump between levels?↓

Mid-to-Senior: 42% ($95K→$135K). Also the level with the most openings.

Do Leads really earn more than Managers?↓

On average, yes. Managers $148K, Leads $155K. IC tracks in engineering and product often match management.

What percentage of jobs are remote?↓

26% remote, 56% on-site, 14% hybrid. Remote pays 35% more ($128K vs $95K median).
