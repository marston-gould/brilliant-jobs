Frequently Asked Questions

Which companies are hiring the most in 2026?↓

Amazon leads with 4,200 open positions, followed by Deloitte (3,100), Google (2,800), Microsoft (2,600), and Meta (2,100). The top 15 employers combined have over 30,000 open roles.

How long do job listings stay open?↓

Most close within 14-30 days. The median is approximately 21 days. Only 320 out of 5,500+ tracked closures lasted 90+ days. Apply within the first two weeks for best odds.

How fast is the job market growing?↓

Discovery peaked at 89,200 new listings in the week of Feb 10 after expanding to 5 ATS platforms. Steady-state discovery is roughly 20,000-35,000 new listings per week.

What is the best time to apply?↓

Within the first 14 days of posting. Our closure data shows the 14-30 day window has the highest rate of listings being taken down, suggesting positions are being filled.
