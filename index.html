The Problem

The job search is rigged.
Just not in your favor.

The biggest job sites are designed to maximize your time on their platform, not minimize your time searching. You already know this. You feel it every time you see "Easy Apply" on a role that was filled six weeks ago.

They show you what employers pay to promote

Sponsored listings get priority. Organic postings get buried. You're not seeing the best jobs — you're seeing the ones with the biggest ad budgets.

Ghost listings stay up because engagement is the product

"Actively Hiring" badges on 90-day-old posts. Roles that were filled weeks ago. They stay up because every click is revenue.

You apply into a black hole with no feedback

No match score. No response tracking. No way to know if your resume was even read. Just silence, and a notification to keep applying.

How it works

When you apply through Brilliant Jobs, we track the outcome. Did you hear back? How long did it take? Did the role close without any response?

Aggregate that across our community and every company gets a public accountability score — visible to you before you apply, and visible to them when they search for themselves.

Companies that respond promptly get recognized. Companies that ghost get a public track record. That's never existed before.
