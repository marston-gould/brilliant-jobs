Features

Job search tools built for people
who are tired of the bullshit

Job boards show you listings. We give you a command center with filters, intelligence, and your network — so you can find the right job, not just any job.

Power Filters

Five filter dimensions — WHAT (title, level, department), WHERE (city radius, state, remote), WHO (company, size), WHEN (posted date), HOW MUCH (salary). Combine them like a pro.

Salary Extraction

We parse salary ranges buried in job descriptions — including multi-zone postings where companies list different ranges for different regions. See the full pay picture before you apply.

Network Intelligence

Your LinkedIn connections are mapped to every company in our database. See which openings are at companies where you already have an inside connection.

Saved Filters with Counts

Save your searches. See how many new jobs matched in the last 1 day, 7 days, and 30 days. Never miss a new posting in your target search.

Location Radius Search

Search by city with intelligent radius matching — not just text matching. "Portland, OR (12.6 mi)" finds jobs in the metro area, not every mention of Portland worldwide.

Smart Exclusions

Tune out the noise permanently. Exclude companies, locations, title keywords, and industries you never want to see again. Your feed gets smarter every session.

Application Tracking

Track every application from click to offer. Manual logging, auto-detection from LinkedIn Easy Apply, or email notifications — your choice. Never lose track of where you stand.

Resume Management

Upload multiple resumes and associate each one with specific saved filters. SEO resume for SEO searches. Marketing resume for marketing. The right version goes with the right job.

Visual Pipeline

Kanban-style board showing every active application by stage: Applied → Screening → Interview → Offer. Drag to update. Spot bottlenecks at a glance.

Resume vs. Job Spec Match

Side-by-side comparison showing how your resume stacks up against a specific job description. See what's missing, what's strong, and what to tweak before you apply.
