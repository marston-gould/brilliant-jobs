How it works

Connect your Gmail and Calendar and we detect what happens after you apply — confirmations, interview invites, rejections, and silence. Your pipeline updates automatically.

When a company closes a role you applied to without ever responding, we tag them. When they respond quickly, we note that too. Over time, every company builds a public track record based on real applicant experiences.

You see this data before you apply. Companies that ghost get a visible record. That changes the incentive.

How It Works

Set up once. It gets smarter
every time you use it.

Three minutes to launch. Then the system learns from everything you do.

01

Upload a resume

Drop in your PDF or DOCX. We read it and auto-generate search filters from your skills, titles, and locations — no manual entry.

02

Connect your accounts

Install the extension and link Gmail, Calendar, and Drive. We detect interview invites, application confirmations, and recruiter replies automatically.

Common Questions

You might be wondering...

Is my data safe?

We never sell your data. Your search filters, resumes, and application history are encrypted and visible only to you. We make money from paid plans and credit packs, not from selling your information to recruiters.

Why not just use LinkedIn?

LinkedIn shows you the jobs that employers pay to promote. We show you every job posted on 39,000+ company career pages — including the ones that never make it to LinkedIn. Plus salary data and ghost rate tracking that LinkedIn will never offer.

How does the free trial work?

You get 7 days of full access — no credit card required. That includes all search and filter tools, pipeline tracking, ghost detection, and company intelligence. You also get AI credit samples: 2 resume tailorings, 2 cover letters, 2 auto-applies, and 1 interview practice session. After 7 days, choose Starter (20/mo, 100 credits) or Pro (40/mo, 300 credits) to keep going. Your pipeline and saved data stay accessible in read-only mode if you don't upgrade.

How current are the job listings?

Jobs are refreshed on a rolling schedule — most boards are checked every 24-48 hours. Stale listings are flagged automatically so you're not wasting time on ghost jobs.
