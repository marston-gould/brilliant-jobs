Common Questions

Is my data safe?

We never sell your data. Your search filters, resumes, and application history are encrypted and visible only to you. We make money from paid plans and credit packs, not from selling your information to recruiters.

Why not just use LinkedIn?

LinkedIn shows you the jobs that employers pay to promote. We show you every job posted on 39,000+ company career pages — including the ones that never make it to LinkedIn. Plus salary data and ghost rate tracking that LinkedIn will never offer.

What does the free plan include?

One saved search, full job feed access, salary data, and ghost rates. Paid plans unlock AI resume scoring, more saved searches, credits for AI features, and priority notifications.

How it works

Connect your Gmail and Calendar and we detect what happens after you apply — confirmations, interview invites, rejections, and silence. Your pipeline updates automatically.

When a company closes a role you applied to without ever responding, we tag them. When they respond quickly, we note that too. Over time, every company builds a public track record based on real applicant experiences.

You see this data before you apply. Companies that ghost get a visible record. That changes the incentive.
