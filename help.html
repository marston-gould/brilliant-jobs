Getting Started

Installation

  1. Create an account at brilliantjobs.app with your email, password, and professional profile URL. Your account will be reviewed before activation.
  2. Install the extension — download the Chrome extension and load it as an unpacked extension via chrome://extensions with Developer Mode enabled.
  3. Log in — open the extension side panel and log in with your email and password. The panel unlocks once your account is approved.
Tip: Pin the extension to your toolbar for easy access. You'll see a blue "B" icon that fills solid when you're on a networking page.

How It Works

Brilliant Jobs helps you discover where your professional connections work, so you can find job opportunities at companies where you already have an inside contact. Here's the workflow:

Step 1: Harvest Connections

Go to the Harvest tab and click "Harvest Connections." The tool navigates to your connections page and collects everyone in your network. For large networks (2,000+), this takes a few minutes as it clicks through all the pages.

The harvester stores names, profile slugs, and any visible company information. You can re-run it anytime to pick up new connections — it won't overwrite profiles that have already been scanned.

Step 2: Scan Profiles

The Scan tab visits each connection's profile to extract their full work history — current and past employers, titles, and company pages. This is where the real data comes from.

Scanning runs automatically at a safe, metered pace:

SettingRange
Profiles per day40 – 90 (randomized daily)
Profiles between breaks8 – 25
Time between visits15 – 45 seconds
Break duration1 – 8 minutes
Why so slow? Professional networking platforms monitor automated activity. Randomized pacing with realistic delays mimics natural browsing behavior. For a network of 2,500 connections, expect scanning to take about 4-6 weeks.

The scanner picks up automatically each day — no need to restart it. You'll see a progress indicator showing visited/queued counts, today's progress, and an estimated completion date.

Step 3: Search Jobs

The Jobs tab scrapes job listings from search results pages. Navigate to a job search, then click "Scrape All Pages" to collect all listings across every page of results. Export as TSV for analysis.

Step 4: Export Data

The Data tab lets you export everything as TSV files — connections with their work history, and all discovered companies. Open in Excel or Google Sheets for filtering and analysis.

Features

Smart Resuming

The scanner remembers exactly where it left off. If you close Chrome, restart your computer, or walk away for a few days, it resumes automatically the next time you open the browser. The daily limit resets each morning between 8-10 AM.

Chrome Notifications

You'll receive desktop notifications when:

  • A new day starts and the scanner resumes
  • The daily profile limit is reached
  • All profiles have been scanned

Dynamic Icon

The toolbar icon changes based on your active tab. Solid blue "B" when you're on a networking page, outline "B" on other sites. A quick visual indicator that the extension is context-aware.

Account & Security

Why do we ask for your profile URL?

We verify your professional profile during signup to prevent abuse and ensure our users are real professionals. Your profile URL is checked during the approval process and is never shared.

What data do we store?

Your connection names, profile slugs, headlines, and work history (companies, titles) are stored in our database. This data is tied to your account and is only accessible by you. We do not sell or share your data.

Password Reset

If you forget your password, use the "Forgot your password?" link on the login page. You can also reset it from the main login page.

Troubleshooting

The scanner shows "0 in queue" but I have thousands of connections
You need to run Harvest first (Tab ①) before scanning. The scanner only processes connections that have been harvested.
Harvest is stuck at a small number and not finding more connections
Make sure you're on the correct connections page and that the page is fully loaded. The harvester clicks "Load More" to paginate through your full list.
The scanner stopped and says "Done for today"
This is normal. The scanner has a randomized daily limit (40-90 profiles) to stay under the radar. It will automatically resume the next morning.
I see "Invalid login credentials" in the extension
Make sure you've confirmed your email (check for a confirmation link in your inbox) and that your account has been approved. If you've forgotten your password, reset it at brilliantjobs.app.
The extension shows "Pending Approval"
New accounts require manual approval. This typically happens within 24 hours. If it's been longer, contact us.
My data disappeared after reinstalling the extension
Your data is stored in the cloud and tied to your account. Log in again after reinstalling and your counts will reappear. Local state (like today's visit count) resets on reinstall, but no data is lost.
Important: Never run multiple scanning tools simultaneously on the same networking platform. This significantly increases the risk of account restrictions.
