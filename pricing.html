Questions

What are credits?
Credits power AI features like resume scoring (3 credits), AI filter generation (2 credits), smart job alerts (1 credit), and AI resume rewrites (5 credits). Free users can explore the platform but need a paid plan to access AI features.
How does the duration discount work?
Choose how many months you want to commit to. Longer commitments get deeper discounts — up to 40% off at 12 months. You pay upfront for the full period and get the discounted rate for every month. If you find a job early, your remaining time stays active so you can use the platform for salary negotiation, offer comparison, and keeping your pipeline warm.
Can I change plans anytime?
You can upgrade your tier (Free → Starter → Pro) anytime and get immediate access to the new features. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current commitment period. Duration extensions can be added at the current or better rate.
Do unused credits roll over?
Included monthly credits reset each billing cycle. Purchased credit packs never expire and carry over month to month.
What happens if I run out of credits?
You can buy credit packs anytime, or enable auto-refill to automatically purchase more when your balance drops below a threshold you set. The rest of the platform — job feed, filters, pipeline — works without credits.
Is there a free trial?
The Free plan is forever free with full access to the job feed, saved filters, and visual pipeline. It's not a trial — it's a permanent tier. Upgrade only when you want AI features and more capacity.
