Methodology

Market dynamics data visualizes weekly trends across 315,000+ active job listings from 39,000+ company career pages. Industry × Department and Department × Level heatmaps show job volume by category per ISO week, with departments classified by title keyword matching and industries assigned via company profile enrichment. Geographic visualizations use state-level location data extracted from job listings (174,000+ with US state codes). Velocity metrics measure week-over-week growth rates by state. All aggregations are pre-computed in Supabase materialized views and cached client-side for 24 hours.
