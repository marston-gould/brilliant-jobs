Methodology

All Market Intelligence Lab data is sourced from 39,000+ company career pages across 5 ATS platforms (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, Recruitee) plus USAJobs federal listings. Job counts, salary figures, department classifications, and geographic distributions are computed from 315,000+ active listings with daily automated refresh cycles. Title-based pattern matching classifies roles by department, seniority level, and work arrangement. Industry assignments use company-level classification from verified employer profiles. All figures reflect currently open positions unless otherwise noted.
