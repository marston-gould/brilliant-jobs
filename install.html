B Install the Chrome Extension

Before you start: You need a Brilliant Jobs account and Google Chrome (or a Chromium-based browser like Edge, Brave, or Arc). If you don't have an account yet, sign up here — it takes 30 seconds.

Download Your Extension

1

Log in to your dashboard

Go to brilliantjobs.app/dashboard and sign in with your account.

2

Navigate to Setup

Click Setup in the left sidebar. The Chrome Extension card is at the top of the page.

3

Download your extension

Click the Download Extension button. This downloads a .zip file that is unique to your account — it contains your personalized security parameters.

Do not share your extension file. It is tied to your account and contains credentials specific to you.

Install in Chrome

4

Unzip the download

Find the downloaded .zip file and extract it to a folder you'll keep (e.g., Documents/brilliant-jobs-ext). Don't delete this folder after installing — Chrome needs it to stay.

5

Open Chrome Extensions

Type chrome://extensions in your address bar and press Enter. Toggle Developer mode on in the top-right corner.

6

Load the extension

Click Load unpacked in the top-left corner. Select the folder you unzipped in Step 4.

7

Pin and open

Click the puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin Brilliant Jobs. You should see the B icon appear in your toolbar.

You're all set. Click the extension icon to open the side panel. Your dashboard and extension will sync automatically.

Updating the Extension

When a new version is available, your dashboard's Setup page will show an update banner with a version comparison. To update:

  • Download the new extension from Setup
  • Unzip it to the same folder (overwrite the old files)
  • Go to chrome://extensions and click the refresh icon on the Brilliant Jobs card

Troubleshooting

Extension not loading? Make sure Developer mode is on, and that you selected the folder that contains manifest.json (not a parent folder).

Side panel not opening? Click the Brilliant Jobs icon in the toolbar. If you don't see it, click the puzzle icon and make sure the extension is enabled and pinned.

Not syncing with dashboard? Refresh both the extension side panel and your dashboard tab. Check that you're signed into the same account in both.

Need more help? Visit the Chrome Extension Guide or reach out via the feedback form in your dashboard.
