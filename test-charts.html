Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average salary for remote jobs in 2026?
Based on 23,000+ job listings with published salary data, the median salary for remote positions is $128,000, compared to $118,000 for hybrid roles and $95,000 for on-site positions. Remote roles consistently pay 15-35% more across most industries and seniority levels.
Which industries are hiring the most in 2026?
Technology leads with approximately 24% of all job listings, followed by Healthcare (11%), Financial Services (10%), and Professional Services (8%). These four industries account for over half of all open positions tracked across 9,800+ company career pages.
What percentage of jobs are remote vs on-site in 2026?
Approximately 56% of listings are on-site, 26% are fully remote, and 14% are hybrid. Remote job availability varies significantly by industry — Technology has the highest remote percentage at roughly 40%, while Manufacturing and Healthcare remain predominantly on-site.
How does salary change by seniority level?
Salary scales dramatically with seniority: Entry Level averages $58K, Mid-Level $95K, Senior $135K, Director $185K, VP $245K, and C-Suite $320K. The largest single jump occurs between Mid-Level and Senior levels, where average compensation increases by approximately $40K (+42%).
Where does Brilliant Jobs get its job market data?
Brilliant Jobs aggregates listings directly from company career pages via 5 major ATS (Applicant Tracking System) platforms: Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, and Recruitee. This covers 9,800+ company career pages and 280,000+ active listings, refreshed every 10 minutes. Unlike job boards, this data comes straight from the source — the actual company career page.
