Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average salary for remote vs on-site jobs in 2026?↓

Remote positions have a median salary of $128,000 compared to $95,000 for on-site — a 35% premium. Hybrid falls between at $118,000. Consistent across industries and levels.

Which industries are hiring the most in 2026?↓

Technology leads with 68,400 positions (24%), Healthcare 31,200 (11%), Financial Services 28,500 (10%). These three = nearly half of all postings.

What salary can I expect at each career level?↓

Entry $58K, mid-level $95K, senior $135K, director $185K, VP $245K, C-suite $320K. Biggest jump: mid-to-senior (42% increase).

How does Brilliant Jobs collect this data?↓

We scan 9,800+ company career pages via 5 ATS platforms (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, Recruitee) every 10 minutes, bypassing job boards entirely.

Which departments pay the most?↓

Engineering $142K median, Product $138K, Data & Analytics $132K, Design $125K. Sales has widest variance due to commissions ($42K-$210K range).

Which US regions have the most job postings per capita?↓

West Coast (SF/LA) leads at 158 jobs per 100K residents, Mid-Atlantic North (NYC/Philly) at 132, and Northwest (Seattle/Portland) at 123. The Heartland has just 48 per 100K despite having 28M residents.

What metro area has the highest concentration of job openings?↓

San Francisco-San Jose DMA leads at 404 jobs per 100K people — 2.5x New York’s density. Seattle (229), Austin (228), Boston (201), and Denver (198) round out the top 5.
