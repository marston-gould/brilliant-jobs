Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average salary for remote vs on-site jobs in 2026?↓

Remote positions have a median salary of $128,000 compared to $95,000 for on-site — a 35% premium. Hybrid falls between at $118,000. This gap is consistent across industries and seniority levels.

Which departments pay the most in 2026?↓

Engineering leads at $142K median, followed by Product ($138K), Data & Analytics ($132K), and Design ($125K). Sales has the widest variance: $42K-$210K at ±2 standard deviations.

What is the most common salary range?↓

The $75K-$100K range has the most listings (4,600 jobs), followed by $100K-$125K (4,140). The $75K-$150K band collectively holds 53% of all salary-listed positions.

What salary can I expect at each career level?↓

Entry $58K, Associate $72K, Mid-Level $95K, Senior $135K, Manager $148K, Lead $155K, Director $185K, VP $245K, C-Suite $320K. Biggest jump: mid-to-senior (42%).

Why do Leads earn more than Managers on average?↓

Senior individual contributor tracks (Lead, Principal, Staff) often match or exceed management tracks in compensation, especially in engineering and product roles where deep technical expertise commands a premium.

Methodology

Salary data is drawn from 49,000+ job listings with disclosed compensation ranges across 39,000+ company career pages. Median salary represents the midpoint of posted min-max ranges. The ±1σ and ±2σ bands are calculated using actual salary range distributions within each category. Department and level classifications use the same title-based pattern matching applied across all Brilliant Jobs analytics. Work arrangement (Remote, Hybrid, On-site) is extracted from job descriptions and location metadata. Salary by industry uses the industry classification from company profiles. All figures reflect currently open positions and refresh daily.
