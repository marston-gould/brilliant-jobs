💼 Brilliant Jobs — Quick Start

How It Works

Brilliant Jobs discovers job opportunities through your professional network. It scans your connections, finds where they work, and helps you find open positions at those companies.

Step-by-Step

1
Harvest — Collects your connections list. Click "Harvest Connections" while on the networking site. Takes a few minutes for large networks.
2
Scan — Visits each connection's profile to extract their work history. Runs automatically at a safe pace (40-90 profiles/day) with built-in breaks.
3
Jobs — Navigate to a job search page and click "Scrape All Pages" to collect listings. Works on search results pages.
4
Data — Export your connections and discovered companies as TSV files for analysis.

Tips

Scanner pacing: The scanner deliberately runs slowly with randomized delays and daily limits to stay under the radar. Don't worry if it takes several days — this is by design.
Re-harvesting: You can run Harvest again anytime to pick up new connections. It won't overwrite data from profiles that have already been scanned.
Keep a tab open: The scanner needs a browser tab available to visit profiles. It will create one if needed, but keeping one open helps.
