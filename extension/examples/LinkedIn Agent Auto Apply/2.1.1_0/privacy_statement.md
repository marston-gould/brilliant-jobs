# Privacy Statement

This extension integrates with CareerGPT to provide automated job application services on LinkedIn.

## Data Usage
- The extension connects to CareerGPT's servers to:
  - Authenticate your CareerGPT account
  - Fetch your saved profile and resume data
  - Track job application status
  - Save your application preferences

## Data Storage and Security
- Your CareerGPT credentials and token are stored securely in your browser
- Form filling preferences are stored locally
- Job application data is stored in your CareerGPT account
- All communication with CareerGPT servers is encrypted

## What this extension does NOT do:
- Does NOT store your LinkedIn credentials
- Does NOT track your general browsing activity
- Does NOT share data with third parties
- Does NOT collect data beyond what's needed for job applications

## Permissions Used
- "activeTab": To interact with LinkedIn job pages
- "storage": To save your preferences locally
- Connection to careergpt.io: To sync with your CareerGPT account

## Data Control
- You can delete your data anytime from your CareerGPT account
- Uninstalling the extension removes all local data
- You can opt out of CareerGPT integration at any time
