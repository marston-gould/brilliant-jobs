Frequently Asked Questions

Which industries are hiring the most in 2026?↓

Technology leads with 68,400 open positions (24%), followed by Healthcare (31,200), Financial Services (28,500), and Professional Services (22,800). These four account for over half of all listings.

Which industries pay the highest salaries?↓

Technology $142K average, Financial Services $138K, Energy $128K, Professional Services $118K. Hospitality is lowest at $65K — 2.2x less than Tech.

What departments have the most openings?↓

Engineering dominates at 72,800 (26%), then Sales 38,400 (14%), Marketing 28,600 (10%). Engineering has more than Sales and Marketing combined.

Is it easier to get hired in Financial Services than Tech?↓

Potentially. Financial Services pays nearly the same ($138K vs $142K) but posts far fewer jobs (28,500 vs 68,400), suggesting each posting may receive fewer applications.

Methodology

Industry data covers 315,000+ active job listings from 39,000+ company career pages across Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, Recruitee, and USAJOBS ATS platforms. Industry classification is derived from company profiles using SIC/NAICS sector mapping enriched with Free Company Dataset data. Job department and function classifications are assigned through title and description keyword matching across 15 standardized categories. Salary figures by industry represent medians of disclosed compensation ranges. Weekly job volume trends track new listings by ISO week. All metrics update daily via materialized database views.
