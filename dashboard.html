Be Brilliant

Find a Brilliant Job

Brilliant Jobs is not another job board.
It's an intelligence platform that scans company hiring pages directly, bypasses job board noise, and gives you a real-time feed of exactly what matches your criteria — before most applicants even know the role exists.
1
Install Your Extension
Head to Setup and download your personalized Chrome extension. It's unique to your account — your own stealth scanner that harvests your LinkedIn network connections and maps them to companies hiring right now. The extension runs in the background while you browse, visiting profiles during business hours at human-like intervals.
2
Build Your Filters
Go to Jobs Feed and open the filter builder. Think of it like building a search radar:
What
Job titles and keywords. "director of marketing", "growth", "SEO"
Not
Exclude noise. "intern", "assistant", "part-time"
Where
Locations and work styles. "United States", "remote", "Seattle"
Who
Target or exclude companies. "Stripe", "Google"
Pro tip: Type or before a term to OR it with the previous pill. "director" then "or VP" matches either. Save each search as a named filter — they run in parallel.
3
Tune Your Results
Getting noise? Head to Tuning to set global rules that apply across all your filters:
Location lock — restrict everything to US-only with one checkbox
Global exclusions — block titles, companies, or locations forever
Level matching — check "Director" and "VP" to soft-match both seniority levels
Poor match analysis — hidden jobs get analyzed for patterns to suggest exclusions
4
Work Your Feed Daily
Each row in your feed has three actions:
Save Bookmark for later — appears in Pipeline
Apply → Opens the ATS page and tracks it as Applied
✕ Hide irrelevant jobs — helps tune future results
Colored number badges next to titles show which saved filter matched that job. Use multi-sort (Shift+Click column headers) to prioritize by Days, Company, or Title.
5
Track Everything in Pipeline
Saved jobs land in the Pipeline Saved column. When you apply, they move to Applied. From there, track Responded → Interview → Offer or Rejected/Ghosted. The stat cards at the top show your response rate and average days to response — the numbers that actually matter.
Your data advantage
Brilliant Jobs currently indexes 110,000+ open positions across 1,900+ companies using Greenhouse ATS — with Lever, Workday, and Ashby coming next. Unlike job boards that show you stale postings, we pull directly from the source. When a company posts a role at 9am, it's in your feed by 9:01am.

Connect your network and integrations

Why do we need a Chrome extension?
Brilliant Jobs scans your LinkedIn network to discover which companies your connections work at, then cross-references those companies with our job database. The result: you see openings where you already have an inside contact who could refer you. This is the single most effective way to get hired — referred candidates are 4x more likely to get an offer.
Coming soon: The extension will also power Ghost Monitor — tracking which companies viewed your profile after you applied, so you know who's ghosting you and who's interested.

Installation Guide
Follow these steps after downloading
1

Unzip the download

Extract the file to a permanent folder on your computer. Don't delete this folder — Chrome needs it.

2

Open Chrome Extensions

Navigate to chrome://extensions and enable Developer mode using the toggle in the top right.

3

Load the extension

Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder. The extension will appear in your toolbar.

4

Pin and open

Click the puzzle piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin the extension for easy access. Then open it to get started.
