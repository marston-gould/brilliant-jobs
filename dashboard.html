This is not another job board.
This is how you take control.
Brilliant Jobs scans 39,000+ company career pages directly — bypassing the noise, the recycled posts, and the ghost listings. Build your search once, and we surface exactly what matches. Same-day. Every day.
Step 1
Install the Chrome Extension
Your extension works quietly in the background, identifying opportunities at companies where you already have professional connections. It maps your network to active hiring — so when a company you're connected to posts a role, you'll know before most applicants do.
Heads up Each extension is unique to your account with its own security parameters. Download it from the Setup page after signing in.
Step 2
Connect Your Accounts
Authorize read-only access so Brilliant Jobs can help track your application pipeline — detecting responses, scheduling interviews, and flagging companies that go silent.
Gmail
Detects confirmations, rejections, and interview requests
Calendar
Tracks interview schedules and follow-up timelines
Drive
Keep resumes synced — edit in Docs, always current here
Why? Gmail and Calendar are the backbone of your application pipeline — they detect confirmations, rejections, interview invites, and ghosting patterns so your dashboard stays current without you updating it manually.
Step 3
Build Your Search Filters
This is where Brilliant Jobs pulls away from everything else. Build a search that no job board can match — combine keywords, locations, salary ranges, seniority levels, and company targets into a single filter. Create as many as you want. Each one runs in parallel, scanning thousands of career pages daily.
What
Job titles and keywords you're targeting
"director of marketing", "growth lead", "SEO"
Not

"intern", "assistant", "part-time"

Locations and work arrangements
"Remote", "Seattle", "United States"
Who
Target or exclude specific companies
"Stripe", "Google", "Not: Amazon"
Tip Type or before a term to add alternatives — "director" then "or VP" matches either. Save each combination as a named filter and they all run simultaneously.
Step 4
Fine-Tune Your Results
Getting noise? Search Tuning gives you global rules that apply across every filter — restrict to US-only, block specific titles or companies permanently, set seniority-level matching, and let our poor-match analysis suggest exclusions based on the jobs you hide.
Step 5
Work Your Feed Daily
Every job in your feed is scored against your resume so you can see your match strength before you invest time. Save jobs to your Pipeline where you can score them against the full JD, then apply in bulk from there. Or use Apply to go directly to the company’s ATS for a single job. Set up auto-apply rules that submit your resume automatically when a job hits a score threshold you choose.
Pipeline Save to your Pipeline to track, score, and apply in bulk
Apply → Apply to this job directly through the company’s ATS
× Hide and improve future results
Pro Upload multiple resumes and assign them to different filters. Brilliant Jobs scores each one against incoming jobs and can customize which resume gets submitted based on the best match — so every application puts your strongest foot forward.
Your data advantage
While job boards recycle stale postings, Brilliant Jobs pulls directly from the source. When a company posts a new role, it lands in your feed the same day — filtered, scored, and matched to your criteria.
And unlike generic market reports, your Stats dashboard shows data about your job market — salary ranges, hiring velocity, top companies, and competition levels based only on roles that match your filters. Not the world's numbers. Yours.

Installation Guide
Follow these 4 steps to get the extension running
1

Unzip the download

Extract the .zip to a permanent folder (like ~/Extensions/brilliant-jobs). Don’t delete it — Chrome needs the folder to keep working.

2

Open Chrome Extensions

Go to chrome://extensions in your address bar and turn on Developer mode (top right toggle).

3

Load the extension

Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder you created in step 1.

4

Pin and open

Click the puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin the Brilliant Jobs extension. Then click it to open.
